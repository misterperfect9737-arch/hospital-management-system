========================================
😰 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
========================================

PROBLEM: App won't run
SOLUTION:
- Stay calm
- Check if you're in right folder
- Try: py app.py
- Say: "Let me restart the application"

PROBLEM: Can't login
SOLUTION:
- Check credentials exactly
- admin / admin123 (no spaces!)
- Say: "Let me verify the credentials"

PROBLEM: Feature not working
SOLUTION:
- Don't panic!
- Say: "This was working earlier, let me check"
- Restart app (Ctrl+C, then py app.py)
- Try again

PROBLEM: Proctor asks unknown question
SOLUTION:
- Be honest!
- Say: "I'm not sure about that"
- Or: "I haven't implemented that feature"
- Don't lie!

PROBLEM: Internet disconnects
SOLUTION:
- Use phone hotspot
- Rejoin meeting quickly
- Say: "Sorry, internet issue. I'm back now"
